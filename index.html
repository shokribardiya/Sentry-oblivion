06 — PRINCIPLES

Principles that guide how we show up.

Capability over credentials.

We care about what you can build, not where you learned to build it. Half of the strongest engineers we know never had a formal title for what they did — they had repositories, papers, proofs and shipped systems.

Hold light and shade.

Every hard problem has a downside worth naming and an upside worth building. We say both out loud, early, and in plain language — before the expensive part.

Do the simple thing that works.

We take an empirical approach: identify the simplest solution that could work, ship it, measure it, iterate. Sophistication is earned, never performed.

Put the work first.

When something urgent needs to be done, the right person to do it is probably you. Ownership beats coordination, and shipping beats discussing shipping.

Communicate kindly and directly.

High trust, low ego. Assume good intentions even in disagreement, say the real thing, and repair quickly when something breaks — including relationships.

Leave the system better.

Code, documentation, culture, onboarding — whatever you touched, it should be easier for the next person. Compounding is the only real advantage.
